FAQ

Do you come to me?

Yes — this is a mobile service. O.G. comes to you and brings his own water supply, so you don't need to provide water or power hookups for the wash.

How does booking through this site work?

Head to the Book a Date tab and open the scheduler — pick your service and a time there. Bookings made through this site require a 50% deposit to hold the date, taken when you book. Deposits are final (non-refundable).

When is payment due?

For detailing, payment is due in full at the end of the job. For videography, payment is due on delivery of the edited video.

Are the prices final?

No — all prices listed are starting prices + tax. Your final quote is confirmed by O.G. once he sees your vehicle or event details.

Is there a deal for first-time clients?

Yes — first-time clients get $25 off their first full detail service. Mention the first-time client deal when you book, and the $25 comes off your final total. One discount per new client.

Do first responders and military get a discount?

Yes — first responders and military service members get 10% off any service, detailing, filming, or editing. Mention it when you book, and the discount comes off your final total.

What photos do I need for a restoration?

For a full restoration, interior photos from all 4 sides are required — driver side, passenger side, rear left, and rear right. Text them to (385) 346-8125 when you book so O.G. can see the job.

What are your hours?

Detailing appointments are available Wednesday, 12:00 PM to midnight, and Thursday–Saturday, 8:00 AM to midnight. Detailing appointments starting after 8:00 PM carry a $50 after-hours fee. Videography has no after-hours fee — instead, video events on Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, or Wednesday morning (before noon) carry a $50 fee.

How far do you travel?

Based in Kearns — service anywhere within a 30-mile radius of Kearns is included. Travel beyond 30 miles adds a travel fee quoted by O.G. — ask when you book.

Can I reschedule?

Reschedules are allowed up to 8 hours before your appointment; anything later needs O.G.'s approval.
