FAQ

Do you come to me?

Yes. This is a mobile service based in . When you book a detail, you choose one option: you can supply water and power, or you prefer Oscar brings his own water and power source. Either option works.

How does booking work?

Tap Book on a service. Detailing asks you to choose water and power before you continue. Then pick card or cash. Card payments add sales tax as its own line. Cash has no tax. The deposit is of that total. Detailing, filming, and editing share one calendar so the appointments don't overlap. If a booking link is still blank, the button texts 385-346-8125 instead.

When is payment due?

For detailing, the balance is due at the end of the job. For videography, the balance is due on delivery of the edited video. The deposit is collected to hold the date.

Are the prices final?

No. Prices on the menu are starting prices and can increase depending on the condition of the vehicle. The menu shows the service price. Tax is added in the booking summary when you choose card.

How is the deposit calculated?

The deposit is of the service price after tax. On a card payment, tax is included first, then the deposit is half of that total. Cash has no tax, so the deposit is half of the service price. The deposit is non-refundable. If you reschedule, it rolls over to the new time.

How late can I cancel?

Cancel at least before the appointment. Reschedules are allowed up to before the appointment; anything later needs Oscar's approval. The deposit rolls over when you reschedule.

Is there a deal for first-time clients?

Yes. First-time clients get off their first full detail service. Mention it when you book, and it comes off the final total. One discount per new client.

Do first responders and military get a discount?

Yes. First responders and military service members get off any service — detailing, filming, or editing. Mention it when you book.

What photos do I need for a restoration?

For a full restoration, interior photos from all 4 sides are required — driver side, passenger side, rear left, and rear right. Text them to 385-346-8125 when you book.

What are your hours?

Detailing appointments are available Wednesday, 12:00 PM to midnight, and Thursday–Saturday, 8:00 AM to midnight. Appointments that start after add a flat after-hours fee to the balance. Videography has no after-hours fee. Video events on Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, or Wednesday morning (before noon) carry a fee.

How far do you travel?

No travel fee within miles of . Anything outside miles has a travel fee. Oscar quotes that fee when you book.

Where do consultations happen?

A free consultation is a 15-minute phone call, or an in-person meeting at a place you choose. The in-person meeting does not use a shop address.
